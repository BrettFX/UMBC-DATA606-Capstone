ATC NER Gold Labeler

Local file: nothing is uploaded. Progress autosaves in this browser (per file name). Load a *_prelabeled.jsonl file produced by scripts/ner/ner_vllm_prototype.py --mode candidates.

Pick a label (1-9), click the first word then the last word of a span (click the same word twice for one word). Click a labeled word to remove its span.

Annotation guidelines

Spans are minimal. COMMAND is the instruction verb (phrase) only, never its numbers or destination. A station name (FACILITY) is separate from its FREQUENCY. A multi-word facility ("rhein radar") is ONE span. Greetings, farewells and filler are never labeled.
